From brief to booked call.

Paste the role. Linkwell does the searching and the ranking, your recruiters do the judging and the talking. Everything lands in one portal, so nothing lives in a spreadsheet or someone's inbox.

1

Find and rank

From a job description, Linkwell searches licensed people data for the market you name and returns the best 25 to 100 people, ranked against your brief.

  • Role, domain, seniority, skills and time in role, scored separately
  • A one-line reason and flags on every candidate
  • Off-title and out-of-area profiles dropped before you see them
2

Review and shortlist

Each candidate has a full profile in the portal: career history, skills matched to the role, education and whether a business email exists.

  • Shortlist or reject in one click
  • Pipeline view per role: new, shortlisted, contacted, replied, booked
  • A home page that shows what needs you today
3

Reach and track

Queue your shortlist for outreach. The Chrome extension drafts a personal message on each LinkedIn profile, and every send and reply is logged back to the candidate.

  • Connection notes, InMail, messages and email
  • Sent from your recruiters' own accounts, one person at a time
  • Replies, notes and follow-up drafts in one timeline
