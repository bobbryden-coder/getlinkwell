How Linkwell uses your Google account

Linkwell is a recruiting tool. It helps recruiters find candidates for the roles they are filling, write personal outreach emails, and send those emails from their own Gmail. Here is exactly what it does with Google.

Sign in with Google

We receive your name and email address so you can sign in to the Linkwell portal. Nothing else.

Send email from your Gmail (optional)

If you connect Gmail, Linkwell sends the outreach emails you have reviewed and approved, from your own address. It never sends anything you have not approved.

See who replied (optional)

Linkwell only looks at the email threads with people you contacted through it, so it can show who answered and stop follow-ups to them. It does not read the rest of your inbox.

  • Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models.
  • Linkwell only works with text. It does not create, edit or share images or video of anyone.
  • You can disconnect Gmail at any time from the portal, and ask us to delete your data at bob@linkwellhq.com.
  • Linkwell's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Full details are in our privacy policy.
