Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Linkwell is a recruiting platform. Recruiters use it to find candidates for their open roles, review them, write tailored outreach emails, send those emails from their own Gmail, and see who has replied. It is made up of the Linkwell web app (portal.linkwellhq.com), a companion browser extension, and an integration for AI assistants.

This policy explains what information Linkwell handles about recruiters who use it and about the candidates they look at, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how long it is kept.

Information about Linkwell users (recruiters)

  • Account details: your name and email address, received from Google when you sign in.
  • What you put into Linkwell: job postings and job specifications you upload, notes, shortlists, candidate statuses, and the outreach emails you write or edit.
  • Connected Gmail account: if you choose to connect Gmail, see "Gmail and Google user data" below.
  • Usage analytics: product events such as signing in, running a search, or sending an email, used to improve Linkwell. These never include the text of your emails.

Information about candidates

To help recruiters find people for a role, Linkwell processes professional information about candidates:

  • Professional profile data such as name, current and past roles, employers, education, skills, location and a public profile link. This comes from licensed people-data providers, and from professional profiles a recruiter chooses to save with the browser extension.
  • Work contact details such as a work email address, found through a contact-data provider. A personal email or phone number is looked up only when a recruiter specifically requests it for that candidate.
  • Outreach and replies: the emails a recruiter sends to the candidate through Linkwell, and the candidate's replies to those emails.

This information is used only to help the recruiter assess the candidate for a role and contact them about it. We process it on the basis of legitimate interests in professional recruitment. Any candidate can ask us what we hold about them, or ask us to delete it, using the contact details below.

Gmail and Google user data

Connecting Gmail is optional. If you connect it, Linkwell asks Google for permission to:

  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send): to send the outreach emails and follow-ups you write or approve in Linkwell, from your own address, to the candidates you choose. Linkwell never sends an email you have not written or approved.
  • Read your email (gmail.readonly): to see whether candidates replied to the outreach you sent. Linkwell looks only at the threads of emails you sent to candidates (including an outreach email you sent yourself from Gmail and marked as sent in Linkwell, which we find in your Sent folder by the candidate's address and subject). It checks those threads for replies, out-of-office notices and bounces, and stores the candidate's reply text so you can see it next to the candidate. Linkwell does not read, store or analyse any other email in your mailbox.

What we store from Gmail: your Gmail address, an access token (encrypted with AES-256), the Gmail message and thread IDs of the emails Linkwell sent or tracked, and the text of candidates' replies to them. To sort a reply (for example, interested or not interested), its text is sent to our AI provider, which processes it under terms that do not allow it to be used to train their models.

Linkwell's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to build user profiles for any other purpose.
  • It is not transferred to anyone except as needed to provide these features, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • No person at Linkwell reads your Gmail data unless you ask us to (for example, to help with a problem), it is needed for security purposes, or the law requires it.
  • It is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine learning models.

You can disconnect Gmail at any time in Linkwell, or by removing Linkwell's access in your Google Account permissions. When you disconnect, we revoke and delete the stored token.

Who we share information with

Linkwell does not sell personal information. We share it only with the providers that run the service:

  • Google: sign-in, and Gmail if you connect it.
  • Vercel: hosts Linkwell's web app and servers.
  • Our database host: stores account, job and candidate records.
  • OpenAI: AI processing to score candidates against a role, write email drafts, read uploaded job specs, and sort replies.
  • Crustdata: professional profile data used to find candidates.
  • FullEnrich: work email lookups, and personal email or phone lookups when a recruiter requests them.
  • PostHog (EU): product usage analytics.
  • Stripe: payments, if you subscribe.

How long information is kept

  • Your account and the jobs, candidates, notes and emails in it: until you delete them or close your account.
  • Gmail token: until you disconnect Gmail or close your account.
  • Candidate records: for as long as the recruiter keeps them on a job, or until the candidate asks us to delete them.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Gmail tokens are encrypted at rest. Access to production systems is limited to the people who run Linkwell.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under UK and EU data protection law, you can ask to access, correct or delete your information, object to its use, or ask for a copy. Email us and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Linkwell changes. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page.
